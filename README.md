🍽️ Food Waste Management Technology
📌 Project Overview

Food Waste Management Technology is a web-based system designed to reduce food wastage by connecting hotels and restaurants with NGOs such as orphanages and old age homes.
The platform ensures surplus food is redistributed efficiently before it expires using real-time tracking and smart matching.

🔄 Project Workflow
1️⃣ User Registration & Login

Users register as:

Donor (Hotel / Restaurant)

NGO (Food Receiver)

Secure login system with role-based dashboards

2️⃣ Surplus Food Entry (Donor)

Donors add surplus food details:

Food name and type

Quantity

Expiry time

Pickup location

Food status is marked as Available

3️⃣ Real-Time Expiry Monitoring

Countdown timer starts after food entry

Color indicators show freshness level:

🟢 Fresh

🟡 Expiring Soon

🔴 Near Expiry

Expired food is automatically removed from availability

4️⃣ NGO Matching & Discovery

NGOs view available food nearby

Food details and remaining time are visible

5️⃣ Food Claim Process (NGO)

NGOs claim suitable food

Status updates:

Available → Claimed

Donor receives real-time notification

6️⃣ Pickup & Distribution

NGO schedules pickup with donor

Food is collected and distributed

Status updates to Completed

7️⃣ Tracking & History

Donors track food donated and saved

NGOs track food received

System maintains complete donation records
